Study, but fun.

A full redesign of the Pajji Learn ICSE platform — landing, auth, dashboard, study, library, leaderboard, membership, settings, Focus Flight and the AdminHub. Same engine, warmer skin: it rewards effort, keeps the room calm, and makes a 14-year-old want to open it before the exam.

Direction

Four rules the whole system obeys
01

Fun on the surface

Stickers, mascot nudges, XP bursts and a streak flame that actually reacts. Delight is the hook that gets a student to open the app on a Tuesday night.

02

Studious underneath

One clear next action per screen, progress you can read in a glance, and nothing that competes with the work. The chrome gets out of the way.

03

Reward the effort

XP for showing up, not just for winning. Streaks, levels and badges celebrate consistency — the behaviour that actually moves marks.

04

Tactile, not noisy

Buttons have a physical press, cards lift a few pixels, one accent per idea. Depth does the talking; no glow soup.

Handoff notes

What changed, and why

Killed the floating draggable status pill (it covered content and was undiscoverable) — status now lives in a fixed ribbon on desktop and a chip on mobile.
